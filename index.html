04 · SWOT analysis

Understanding myself to improve myself.

Self-awareness helps me recognise where I stand and what I need to work on.

＋ STRENGTHS

Self-awareness. Curiosity and a desire to understand things deeply. Willingness to reflect on my own thinking and behaviour.

↗ WEAKNESSES

I process before I speak, sometimes for too long. I need to become more comfortable expressing my thoughts without overthinking.

✦ OPPORTUNITIES

Focus more on practical application. Teach others what I learn, write and share my understanding, and work with deadlines that encourage action.

△ THREATS

Remaining a learner without becoming a practitioner or builder. Spending too much time understanding ideas without converting them into practical outcomes.

My reflection: Self-awareness matters only when it leads to self-improvement. Understanding my weaknesses is the first step; working on them is the real achievement.

06 · My ideal person

Inspired by depth, dedication and passion.

♫
Nusrat Fateh Ali KhanMUSIC · DEDICATION · DEPTH

A lesson in choosing depth.

My ideal person is the legendary Qawwali maestro Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan. What inspires me most is his dedication to his art and his commitment to exploring the depth of Qawwali.

His journey represents an important lesson for me: choosing depth over the safer or easier path. Despite his father's preference for a conventional profession such as medicine or engineering, he followed his passion for music.

What I learn from him

Choose depth over distraction. Follow your passion with dedication. Excellence comes from consistent practice. Don't simply follow expectations; understand what genuinely motivates you.

“Don't try to do everything. Choose something meaningful, go deeper and give it your best.”
